Size & seed
Multiples of 64 convert to whole WorldBox chunks (the converter rounds to 64). Number boxes are editable.
Land shape
Continents come from simulated tectonic plates: colliding plates raise mountain ranges, ocean–land subduction makes coastal ranges and island arcs, and pulling-apart zones sink into rifts.
✏️ Edit map
Pick a tile, then tap the map. Bucket fills a connected area; Brush paints; Pick copies a tile from the map; Pan lets you scroll when zoomed in. Generating again replaces your edits.
